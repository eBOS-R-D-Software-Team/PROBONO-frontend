Scenario C build summary

Assumptions
- Scenario C builds on the already filtered/calmed Scenario A demand files and the already relocated Scenario A bus stops.
- The school-frontage corridor between Rue du Vieux Moulin and Clos du Bergoje is approximated as a shared-space zone by capping 12 core corridor edges at 5.56 m/s (20 km/h).
- Pedestrians retain multiple entry points into the shared-space zone through distributed low-priority crossings across the frontage band and at both side-street entries, while the longer corridor links remain crossing-based in the operational KPI build.
- A detector-aligned school-frontage pedestrian path is retained at the school sensor proxy so pedestrians visibly cross through the shared-space frontage where school access activity is most expected without forcing a full carriageway split in the vehicle network.
- Additional west/east frontage path proxies were tested under the full sensor-based pedestrian demand but not retained in the official KPI build because they destabilized vehicle operations; the stable Scenario C proxy keeps one detector-aligned school-frontage crossing plus the other distributed shared-space crossings.
- SUMO's sublane model is enabled with lateral-resolution 1.2 m to approximate the unmarked, mixed-surface character of the shared space more faithfully than rigid lane-following.
- Literal frontage-walk trajectories on the carriageway were tested but not retained in the final KPI build because they destabilized the corridor in SUMO; the stable proxy keeps pedestrian freedom concentrated at multiple unsignalized shared-space crossings instead.
- Corridor pedestrian connectors are retained at 5 key nodes and prepared as low-priority shared-space crossings/path proxies so pedestrians can cross the frontage band at several points without relying on zebra-style signal control.
- Traffic signal 2213731092 is retained in the operational KPI build as a stability proxy under the full sensor-based pedestrian demand, while the rest of the corridor is still modeled as low-speed shared space.
- Parking removal is inherited from the existing scenario files; no parkingArea or parkingRoadside objects were present to remove in the builder.
- Raised materials, curb removal, lighting changes, and surface coloring are not explicit SUMO objects, so they are represented only through speed and control changes.
- The narrative idea of pedestrians crossing literally anywhere is approximated through multiple low-priority crossings distributed across the zone rather than longitudinal carriageway walking, which proved to be the most stable SUMO proxy for this corridor.
